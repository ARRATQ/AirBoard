# Resources

The [Resources](/resources) page gathers the applications, documents and links made available to you, sorted by category. You only see the resources open to your groups.

## Opening a resource

1. Open **Resources** from the menu.
2. Find the category, or type a name in the search box.
3. Click the tile: the resource opens, often in a new tab.

The **i** button on a tile shows its description before you open it.

## Keeping your resources at hand

- Click the **star** on a tile to **Add to favorites**.
- Your favourites appear on the home page (**Favorite resources**) and at the top of the Resources page (**My Favorites**).
- Click the star again to **Remove from favorites**.

## Sorting and filtering

- **Resource type**: **All**, **Apps**, **Documents**, **Sites and links**.
- **Display**: **Grid**, **List** or **Table**.
- **Expand All** / **Collapse All**: opens or closes every category.

> **Good to know**: the **New** badge marks a recently added resource. The **Private** badge marks a resource reserved for one of your groups.

> **A resource is missing?** Ask the person who manages your group's resources, or the administrator.
