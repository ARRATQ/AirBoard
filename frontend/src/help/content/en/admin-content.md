# Announcements, media and content settings

## Announcements

An announcement shows as a banner on the home page (information, alert…).

1. Open [Announcements](/admin/announcements) and click **New Announcement**.
2. Give a **Title**, a **Content** and a **Type**: **Information**, **Warning**, **Success** or **Error**.
3. Set a **Priority** and the period (**Start date**, **End date**).
4. Keep **Active Announcement** ticked, then **Save**.

## Media

[Media](/admin/media) stores the images and documents used in the portal.

- **Upload**: drag and drop a file.
- **Copy URL**: to use it in an article or a resource.
- Filter by **Type** (images, videos, PDF…), **Edit** the name, **Delete**.

## Article categories and types

In **News**, the **Categories** and **Types** tabs (administrator only) define the classification offered to writers: name, icon, colour, order.

## Suggestions

[Suggestions](/admin/suggestions) lists every idea: **Archive** removes a suggestion from the box, **Activate** puts it back. On a suggestion's page, move its **status** forward (Reviewing, Planned, In progress, Delivered, Declined). The **Categories** tab manages the categories offered; deactivate a category already in use rather than deleting it.

## Points and badges

[Gamification](/admin/gamification):

- **Point rules**: points earned per action, and whether the action is active;
- **Badges**: **Create** a badge with its **Trigger event**, its **Progress metric** and its **Unlock threshold**.
