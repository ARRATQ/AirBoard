# Events

The [Events](/events) page shows the organisation's events and public holidays. You see public events and those meant for your groups.

## Viewing events

1. Open **Events** from the menu.
2. Choose the **Display**: **Calendar** or **List**.
3. Click a category to show only its events; **Holidays** shows only public holidays. Click again to show everything.
4. Click an event to see its date, time, **Place** and **External links** (video call, registration…).

## What the labels mean

- **All day**: the event has no set time.
- **Recurring**: it repeats (every day, week, month or year).
- **Past**: it has already taken place.

## Counting working days

The **Working days** calculator, below the calendar, counts the working days between a **Start date** and an **End date**. Click the **Weekend days** to pick your days off, and tick **Exclude holidays from calculation** if needed.

> **Good to know**: **Upcoming events** also appear on your home page. You can comment on an event from its page.
