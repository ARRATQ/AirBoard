# Managing sub-groups

Your role lets you organise user groups from **Administration → My groups**. A member of a sub-group also sees everything meant for the group above.

:::groups
You manage the sub-groups **of your groups**:

- you create, rename and delete sub-groups placed under your groups;
- you only add **members of your groups**;
- your groups themselves are not changed here.
:::

:::all
You manage **every group on the site**: you can also create top-level groups and add any active account.
:::

The default group and directory-linked groups stay with the administrator: they carry the **Managed by the administrator** label.

## Creating a sub-group

1. Open [My groups](/admin/my-groups).
2. Click **New sub-group**, or the **Add a sub-group** icon on the parent group's row.
3. Give a **Name**, and if you like a **Colour** and a **Description**.
4. Check the **Parent group**.
5. Under **Members**, type a name and choose the people.
6. Click **Create the group**.

## Editing or deleting

- **Edit** (pencil): name, colour, parent, members.
- **Delete** (bin): the group disappears; its sub-groups move up a level and its members leave it.

> **Good to know**: adding someone to a sub-group opens up everything that targets the group above. Choose members accordingly.
