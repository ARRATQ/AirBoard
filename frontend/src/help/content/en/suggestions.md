# Suggestion Box

The [Suggestion Box](/suggestions) is where you propose an improvement and follow what happens to it.

## Proposing an idea

1. Open **Suggestion Box** from the menu.
2. Click **New suggestion**.
3. Give a short **Title** and choose a **Category**.
4. In **Description**, explain the need, its impact and, if possible, a solution.
5. Tick **Publish anonymously** if you do not want your name to appear.
6. Click **Send**.

## Backing other people's ideas

1. Open a suggestion (**View details**).
2. Vote **For** or **Against**; **Remove my vote** cancels your choice.
3. Add a comment to clarify or build on the idea.

## Following progress

Each suggestion has a status: **Received**, **Reviewing**, **Planned**, **In progress**, **Delivered** or **Declined**. Filter by **Status** to find ideas that are moving forward.
