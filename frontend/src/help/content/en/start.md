# Finding your way around the portal

The portal brings together your tools, the organisation's news, the calendar and conversations. This guide shows where to find each thing.

## The left-hand menu

- **Home**: your starting page.
- **Resources**, **News Hub**, **Events**, **Polls**, **Suggestion Box**, **My XP**, **Who's Who**, **Search**: one page per module.
- **Administration**: only appears if your role gives you pages to manage. You only see those pages.

On a phone, open the menu with the button at the top of the screen.

## The home page

It sums up what matters to you today:

- **Featured** and the **News feed**: the latest articles;
- **Upcoming events**;
- open **Polls**, with a **Vote** button;
- **Favorite resources** and **Recently added** resources;
- **My XP**: your level and badges.

## Searching

1. Click the **Search the portal** bar at the top of the home page, or press **Ctrl K** (**⌘K** on a Mac).
2. Type at least 2 characters.
3. Filter if needed: **Resources**, **News**, **Events**, **Polls**, **Announcements**.

## Display settings

At the bottom of the menu:

- **Display size** ("Auto · 100 %"): makes the whole portal larger or smaller;
- **Dark mode**: switches between light and dark;
- **Language**: French, English, Spanish or Arabic;
- **Help** (question mark): opens these guides, right on the page you are on.

> **Good to know**: your name, at the bottom of the menu, opens **My Profile** and **Logout**.
