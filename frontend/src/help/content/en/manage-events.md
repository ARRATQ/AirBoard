# Publishing events

Your role lets you announce events in the calendar. They are managed from **Administration → Events**.

:::all
You manage events **for the whole site**: an event can be public (visible to everyone) or reserved for chosen groups.
:::

:::groups
You manage events **for your groups**: each event targets at least one of your groups and is only visible to their members (sub-groups included).
:::

## Creating an event

1. Open [Events](/admin/events) and click **New Event**.
2. Give a **Title** and a **Description**.
3. Under **Date & time**, set the **Start date** and **End date**. Tick **All day** if there is no set time.
4. Give the **Place**: address, room or video-call link.
5. Add **External links** if needed (registration, documents) with **Add link**.
6. Under **Classification**, choose the **Category**, the **Priority** and the **Colour** shown in the calendar.
:::all
7. Under **Visibility**, leave **Public event** for everyone, or untick it and choose groups.
:::
:::groups
7. Under **Visibility**, choose at least one of your groups.
:::
8. Click **Create Event**.

## Repeating events

Tick **This event repeats**, then choose the **Recurrence type** (daily, weekly, monthly, yearly), the **Interval** and the **End of recurrence**.

## Publishing or withdrawing

- **Publish immediately** makes the event visible straight away; otherwise it stays a **Draft** until the **Publication date**.
- In the list, **Publish** / **Unpublish** shows or hides an event without deleting it; **Edit** and **Delete** are next to them.

> **Good to know**: public holidays are imported in one go with **Import holidays** (administrator only).
