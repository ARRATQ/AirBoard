# Polls

[Polls](/polls) collect your opinion. You only see those open to everyone or to your groups.

## Voting

1. Open **Polls** from the menu, or click **Vote** on the home page.
2. Pick your answer:
   - **Single choice**: one option only;
   - **Multiple choices**: several options allowed.
3. Click **Vote**.

While the poll is open, you can **Remove my vote** and vote again.

## Seeing the results

Depending on how the poll is set up, results are visible right away, after you vote, or only once it closes. The message under the poll tells you which.

> **Good to know**: in an **Anonymous** poll, nobody can see who voted for what.

> If you pick the **Other** option, the portal invites you to describe your idea in the **Suggestion Box**.

The **Closing soon** badge and the **Until** date show how long is left to vote.
