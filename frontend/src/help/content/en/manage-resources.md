# Managing resources

Your role lets you manage the resources (applications, documents, links) of the Resources page. They are managed from **Administration → Resources**, in two tabs: **Resources** and **Categories**.

:::all
You manage resources **for the whole site**. A **public** category is visible to the groups the administrator gives access to (Groups page, Access tab). A **private** category belongs to one group: only its members see it.
:::

:::groups
You manage resources **for your groups**. Your categories are **private**: each belongs to one of your groups, and only its members see it (sub-groups included).
:::

## 1. Create a category

A resource always sits in a category: start there.

1. Open the [Categories](/admin/resources/categories) tab and click **New category**.
2. Give a **Category name**, a **Description**, a **Colour** and an **Icon**.
:::all
3. Tick **Private resource category** to reserve it for one group, then choose the **Owner Group**.
:::
:::groups
3. Choose the **Owner Group** among your groups.
:::
4. Click **Create**.

> The **Owner Group** cannot be changed afterwards.

## 2. Add a resource

1. Open the [Resources](/admin/resources) tab and click **New Resource**.
2. Enter the **Resource Name**, its **Resource category** and its full **URL** (with https://).
3. Add a **Description**, a **Colour** and an **Icon** so it is recognised at a glance.
4. Keep **Open in new tab** ticked for an external site.
5. Click **Create**.

## Warning users

The **Note on the tile** (140 characters) is shown on the tile, for example "Maintenance Thursday 6–8 pm". **Show until** removes it automatically on the chosen date.

## Hiding, ordering, deleting

- Untick **Active** to hide a resource or category without deleting it.
- The **Display Order** sets its place in the list.
- Deleting a category **also deletes all its resources**, permanently.
