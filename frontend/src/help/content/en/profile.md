# Profile and notifications

## Updating your profile

1. Click your name at the bottom of the menu, then **My Profile**.
2. Add a **Profile Photo**: **Upload Photo** (JPG, PNG, GIF or WebP, 5 MB maximum).
3. Fill in your **Personal Information** (first name, last name, phone, location) and **Professional Information** (job title, department).
4. Click **Save**.

Your username cannot change. To change your email, contact an administrator.

## Changing your password

1. In **My Profile**, click **Change Password**.
2. Enter the **Current Password**, then the new one twice (at least 8 characters).

> If you sign in with your Microsoft account, your password is managed by Microsoft, not here.

## Notifications

The **bell** at the top of the menu flags what is new for you. The [Notifications](/notifications) page lets you:

- show only **Unread** ones;
- **Mark as read** a notification, or **Mark all as read**;
- **Delete all read** to tidy up.
