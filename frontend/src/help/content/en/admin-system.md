# Settings and monitoring

## Overview

[Overview](/admin) sums up activity: **Key figures** and items **To do**.

## Settings

[Settings](/admin/settings) customises the portal:

- **General**: application name and icon, home titles and messages, number of articles in the news feed, home banner image (light and dark theme);
- **Dynamic Messages**: the messages scrolling in the home banner;
- **Access & authentication**: open sign-up or not, and choose the **default group** for new accounts;
- **Appearance**: the portal's colour palettes;
- **Danger Zone**: database reset. Only use it if you know what you are doing: it erases data.

## OAuth sign-in

[OAuth sign-in](/admin/oauth) sets up sign-in with Microsoft or Google:

1. Click **Configure** for the provider.
2. Paste the **Client ID** and **Client Secret** from the provider's console.
3. Copy the **Redirect URI** into that console.
4. Tick **Enable this OAuth provider**: its button appears on the sign-in page.

## Email

[Email](/admin/email) sets up notification sending: sending server, **Email Templates** and **Sending History**.

## Analytics and reports

- [Analytics](/admin/analytics): clicks on resources, unique users, peak hours, most used resources. **Export data** downloads the figures.
- [Reports](/admin/reports): activity by role, group and user (reads, votes, publications, top contributors).

## Chatbots

[Chatbots](/admin/chatbots) adds assistants to the portal: **New Chatbot**, then the **Name** and the **n8n Webhook URL**. **Activate** / **Deactivate** shows or hides them from users.

> **Good to know**: never paste a secret (password, client secret) anywhere other than its own field.
