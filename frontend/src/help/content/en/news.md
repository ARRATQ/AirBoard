# News

The [News Hub](/news) publishes the organisation's articles. Some are for everyone, others only for your groups.

## Finding an article

1. Open **News Hub** from the menu.
2. Use **Search articles...**, or filter by category, type or tag.
3. Click an article to read it in full.

The **Urgent**, **Important** and **Pinned** badges flag articles not to miss.

## Reacting

At the bottom of an article:

- **Was this helpful?**: thumbs up or down;
- **Comments**: write in **Add a comment...** and click **Publish**.

You can **Edit** or **Delete** your own comments.

> **Good to know**: a comment marked **Pending** becomes visible once moderation approves it.

> A **Poll linked to this article** may appear in the article: you can vote without leaving the page.
