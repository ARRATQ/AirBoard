# Points and badges

[My XP](/gamification) rewards your participation: you earn points (XP), level up and unlock badges.

## Earning points

You earn points by using the portal, for example:

- logging in every day;
- reading an article, opening a resource;
- voting in a poll, commenting;
- proposing or backing a suggestion;
- publishing an article or an event, if your role allows it.

The administrator sets how many points each action earns. **Recent Activity** shows what your latest actions brought you.

## Following your progress

- **Level**: the bar shows the points you still need for the next one.
- **Streak**: number of days in a row you have logged in.
- **Badges**: filter **All**, **Unlocked** or **Locked**; each badge says how to earn it.
- **Leaderboard**: your **rank** among participants.

> **Good to know**: the **My XP** card on the home page sums all this up; **Details** opens this page.
