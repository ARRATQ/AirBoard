# Messaging and assistants

## Writing to a colleague

The round button at the bottom of the screen (showing **CHAT** and **AI** in turn) opens messaging and the assistants. A number shows your unread messages.

1. Click this button, then **Messages**.
2. Look for a person or group in **Search contacts…**.
3. Write in **Write a message…** and click **Send**. The emoji button adds an emoji.

The **Online** dot shows who is connected right now.

## Conversation settings

The **Conversation options** menu lets you:

- turn **Notification sounds** off or on;
- **Clear history** of the conversation.

**Fullscreen** enlarges messaging; **Close** folds it away.

## Assistants

If your organisation offers them, an assistant (chatbot) answers your questions:

1. Click the round button, then **AI assistants**.
2. Choose the assistant, then ask your question.

**Switch assistant** moves from one to another.

> **Good to know**: never share a password or confidential information with an assistant.
