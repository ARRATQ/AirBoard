# Creating polls

Your role lets you ask users for their opinion with polls. They are managed from **Administration → Polls**.

:::all
You manage polls **for the whole site**: with no target group, a poll is open to everyone.
:::

:::groups
You manage polls **for your groups**: each poll targets at least one of your groups, and only their members can vote (sub-groups included).
:::

## Creating a poll

1. Open [Polls](/admin/polls) and click **New Poll**.
2. Write the **Poll Question** and, if useful, a **Description**.
3. Enter the **Response Options**: at least 2, at most 10 (**Add an option**).
4. Choose the **Poll Type**: **Single choice** or **Multiple choices**.
5. Set the **Results Visibility**: **Always visible**, **After voting** or **After closing only**.
6. Tick **Anonymous poll** to hide who voted.
:::all
7. Under **Period and Targeting**, set a **Start date** and **End date** if needed, and **Target groups** (empty = everyone).
:::
:::groups
7. Under **Period and Targeting**, set a **Start date** and **End date** if needed, and choose at least one of your groups in **Target groups**.
:::
8. Click **Create Poll**.

## Advanced options

Click **Show advanced options** to:

- **Mark as Other** on an option: whoever picks it is invited to detail their idea in the suggestion box;
- **Link to article** or **Link to announcement**: the poll is shown in that article or announcement.

## Following and closing

In the list: **See results**, **Edit**, **Close** (nobody can vote any more) or **Delete** (permanent).

> **Good to know**: untick **Active poll** to prepare a poll without opening it to votes.
