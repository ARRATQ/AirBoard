# Groups

Groups decide **who sees what**: resources, and targeted news, events and polls. They are managed from **Administration → Groups**.

## Creating a group

1. Open [Groups](/admin/groups) and click **New group**.
2. **General** tab: **Group name**, **Identification color**, **Description**, and **Parent group** if it is a sub-group.
3. **Access** tab: tick the **resource categories** this group can see.
4. Click **Create group**.

Members are added from each user's form (Users page); the **Members** tab lists them.

## Sub-groups

A group placed under a **Parent group** is a sub-group:

- its members also see what is meant for the parent group;
- a role limited to the parent group also covers the sub-group.

The list shows groups as a tree, with "Sub-group of:".

## Directory-linked groups

A group marked **Directory: …** follows a unit of the organisation: people assigned to that unit are members automatically. The link is set in directory management (see "Managing the directory").

## Default group

The default group brings everyone together. It is chosen in **Settings → Access & authentication**.

> **Good to know**: to let someone organise sub-groups without full rights, give them a role with the **Manage sub-groups** permission.

Deleting a group removes all its memberships; this cannot be undone.
