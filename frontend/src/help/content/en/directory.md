# Who's Who

[Who's Who](/directory) presents the organisation: its units, positions and the people who hold them.

## Finding someone

1. Open **Who's Who** from the menu.
2. Type a name, position, unit or email in the search box, or pick a unit in **All units**.
3. Click a person: their card shows their **Contact** details and **Positions held**.

## Browsing the organisation

- **Expand all** / **Collapse all**: opens or closes every unit.
- **Org chart**: tree view. **Show this unit as the root** focuses on one unit; **Whole organization** goes back to the full view.

> **Good to know**: **Acting** marks a position held temporarily. **Vacant position**: nobody holds it for now.

> Is some information about you wrong? Tell the administrator: they keep the directory up to date.
