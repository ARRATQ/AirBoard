# Publishing news

Your role lets you write articles. They are managed from **Administration → News**.

:::all
You manage news **for the whole site**: your articles are visible to every user, and you can edit or delete any article.
:::

:::groups
You manage news **for your groups**: each article targets at least one of your groups and is only visible to their members (sub-groups included). You can edit your own articles and those that target your groups.
:::

## Writing an article

1. Open [News](/admin/news) and click **New Article**.
2. Give a **Title** and a short **Summary**: it appears in lists and on the home page.
3. Write the **Article Content**. The toolbar formats the text and adds headings, lists, links, images or videos.
4. Under **Classification**, choose the **Category**, the **Type** and the **Priority** (**Normal**, **Important** or **Urgent**).
5. Add **Tags** so the article is easy to find: pick one, or **+ Add a tag** to create one.
:::groups
6. Under **Target Groups**, choose at least one of your groups. Without a group, the article cannot be saved.
7. Set the **Publication**, then click **Create article**.
:::
:::all
6. Set the **Publication**, then click **Create article**.
:::

## Choosing when the article appears

- **Publish immediately**: the article is visible as soon as it is saved.
- Otherwise it stays a **Draft** until the chosen **Publication date**.
- **Expiry date** (optional): the article is archived automatically after this date.

## Editing or removing an article

In the list, find the article (filters **Category**, **Status**, priority), then use **Edit** or **Delete**. Deleting is permanent.

> **Good to know**: to attach a poll to an article, create the poll and pick the article in **Link to article** (poll advanced options).
