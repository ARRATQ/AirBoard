# Moderating comments

Your role lets you moderate comments **across the whole site**. Moderation happens in **Administration → Moderation**.

## Handling pending comments

1. Open [Moderation](/admin/comments), **Pending** tab.
2. For each comment, read the **Content** and the **Commented item**.
3. Choose:
   - **Approve**: the comment becomes visible;
   - **Flag**: it is marked **Flagged** for review;
   - **Delete**: it is removed permanently.

## Acting directly on a page

On an article, an event or a suggestion, you can **Edit** or **Delete** any comment, not just your own.

## Comment settings

The **Settings** tab lets you:

- **Enable comments**, then allow them or not on news, resources and events;
- turn on **Require moderation**: every new comment then waits for your approval;
- set the **Maximum comment length**.

Click **Save Settings** to apply.
