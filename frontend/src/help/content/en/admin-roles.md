# Roles and permissions

The administrator has every right. To hand part of the management to others, create **roles** in **Administration → Roles**.

## Available permissions

| Permission | What it opens |
|---|---|
| **Manage resources** | Resources and their categories |
| **Manage news** | Articles and tag creation |
| **Manage events** | Calendar events |
| **Manage polls** | Create, close and delete polls |
| **Moderate comments** | Moderation across the site |
| **Manage sub-groups** | The "My groups" page |

Each permission has a **Scope**:

- **Whole site**: the person manages the whole module;
- **Their groups**: only what concerns their groups (and their sub-groups).

## Creating a role

1. Open [Roles](/admin/roles) and click **New role**.
2. Give a **Name** (e.g. "Communications") and a **Description**.
3. Tick the **Permissions** and choose the **Scope** of each.
4. Click **Save**.

## Assigning a role

- **Manage members** on the role card: search for people and add them.
- Or, in a user's form, **Role & groups** tab, **Assigned roles** section.

A person can hold several roles: their permissions add up.

> **Good to know**: to limit a role to some of a person's groups, open their user form and set **Groups covered by the role**.

Deleting a role removes its permissions from all its members.
